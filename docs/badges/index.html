Badges

Build for the community

Lay a road, build a bridge, open a shop: an admin awards you a badge for it. Every badge has four tiers, Bronze, Silver, Gold and Diamond, and each tier includes the one before it.

The End opens once every base you can reach over land is connected to spawn by road or bridge.

    The rules

    What counts

    Road
    Connects two points along a clearly laid-out route, wide enough for a horse without obstructions, and mob-proofed with lighting or other methods.
    Bridge
    Spans a gap end to end, connects to roads, fits a horse, is properly lit and has some design features.
    Shop
    Whoever builds, maintains and stocks a shop is a keeper, and several people can keep one shop. It must be connected to the road network and stand in the shopping district.
    Highway
    A road in the Nether, or a large straight tunnel in the Overworld.
    Restored chunk
    Looks better than when it was first loaded: no unusual holes, and some lighting added.
