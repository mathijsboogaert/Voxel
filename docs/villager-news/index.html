VOXEL BADGES ARE HERE! Voxel Admin Mathijs has been a very busy bee indeed this past week and has introduced a new system to the server: Badges! Here is a list of all the current badges and how to get your own: Road Runner, Bridge Builder, Shopkeeper, Game Master, Highway Builder, Public Service Agent and Cleanup Crew.

Lay a road, build a bridge, open a shop, or complete one of the other qualifying activities, and an admin can award you a badge for it.

Every badge has four tiers:

Bronze

Silver

Gold

Diamond

with each tier including the one before it.

But how do you get a badge? Lay a road, build a bridge, open a shop, or complete one of the other qualifying activities, and an admin can award you a badge for it. Every badge has four tiers: Bronze, Silver, Gold and Diamond, with each tier including the one before it.

Road:

A pathway that connects two points along a clearly laid-out route. It should be wide enough for a horse to pass through without obstructions. The road itself must be mob-proofed using lighting or other methods.

Bridge:

Spans a gap from end to end and connects to roads. It must be wide enough for a horse to cross and properly lit. The bridge should also have at least some decorative/design features.

Shop:

If you build, maintain, and stock a shop, you are considered a shopkeeper. Multiple people can run a single shop. The shop must be connected to the road network and located in the Shopping District.

Highway:

A road in the Nether or a large, straight tunnel in the Overworld.

Restored Chunk:

A chunk that looks better than it did when it was first loaded. There should be no unusual holes, and some lighting should be added where needed.

If you think you’re eligible for a badge, please let Mathijs know and he’ll award you the badge and add you to the website!
